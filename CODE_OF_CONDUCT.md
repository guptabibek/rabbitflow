# Community conduct

Be respectful and specific. Welcome questions, credit others' work, discuss ideas without personal attacks, and respect privacy. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, pause a discussion, warn participants or restrict participation proportionately. Explain moderation actions privately when possible and allow an appeal to an uninvolved maintainer when available.

For confidential conduct reports, email [guptabibek166@gmail.com](mailto:guptabibek166@gmail.com). Include the relevant project interaction, your concern and a safe way to contact you; share only the personal information needed to investigate. The owner approved publication of this address. Reports are handled privately on a need-to-know basis. No response-time SLA is promised; a backup moderator still needs to be designated. GitHub's platform reporting tools remain available for platform abuse. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and project events.
