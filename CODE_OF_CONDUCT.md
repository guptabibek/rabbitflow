# Community conduct

Be respectful and specific. Welcome questions, credit others' work, discuss ideas without personal attacks, and respect privacy. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, pause a discussion, warn participants or restrict participation proportionately. Explain moderation actions privately when possible and allow an appeal to an uninvolved maintainer when available.

A private conduct-reporting contact has not yet been designated. Until the owner adds one, do not put personal information in public reports; use GitHub's platform reporting tools for abuse. Establishing a confidential project contact and backup moderator is a launch prerequisite. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and project events.
